# Customer Complaints Handling

## Intake Channels

Customers can submit complaints through the support portal, support email, or by phone. All channels
feed into a single ticketing system so complaints are tracked consistently regardless of how they were
received. Every complaint is acknowledged within 4 business hours of receipt.

## Severity Tiers and Response SLAs

Complaints are triaged into four severity tiers. P1 (service outage or data loss affecting the
customer) requires a response within 1 hour and a resolution plan within 4 hours. P2 (major feature
broken, no workaround) requires a response within 4 hours and resolution within 2 business days. P3
(minor issue with a workaround available) requires a response within 1 business day and resolution
within 5 business days. P4 (general feedback or feature requests) requires acknowledgment within 2
business days with no fixed resolution SLA.

## Escalation Matrix

P1 and P2 complaints are automatically escalated to the on-call engineering lead and the customer's
account manager. Repeat complaints from the same customer within 30 days are escalated to the Customer
Success leadership team for a relationship review. Complaints alleging billing errors are routed
directly to Finance in parallel with the standard support flow.

## Root-Cause Documentation and Trend Review

Every P1 or P2 complaint requires a written root-cause note before the ticket can be closed, even if
the immediate symptom is resolved. The Customer Success team reviews complaint trends monthly, looking
for repeat root causes across multiple customers, and shares findings with Product and Engineering to
prioritize fixes.
