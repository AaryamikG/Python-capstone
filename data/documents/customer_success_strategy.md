# Customer Success Strategy

## Onboarding Milestones

New customers follow a 30/60/90 day onboarding plan. By day 30, the customer should have completed
initial setup and core-feature training. By day 60, they should be using the product for their primary
use case with minimal support tickets. By day 90, the customer success manager conducts a formal
check-in to confirm the customer has reached their stated goals and to identify expansion opportunities.

## Customer Health Score

Each account is assigned a health score computed from product usage frequency, support ticket volume
and severity, NPS survey responses, and contract renewal proximity. Health scores are reviewed weekly;
accounts that drop into the "at risk" band trigger an automatic alert to the assigned customer success
manager.

## Churn-Risk Indicators

Early-warning indicators for churn risk include a sustained drop in login frequency, a spike in support
tickets tagged "frustration" or "considering alternatives," non-response to renewal outreach, and loss
of the original champion contact at the customer organization. When two or more indicators appear
within the same 30-day window, the account is flagged for proactive outreach from both the customer
success manager and a sales leader.

## Quarterly Business Reviews and Expansion

Strategic accounts receive a quarterly business review (QBR) covering usage trends, ROI achieved, and
upcoming roadmap items relevant to the customer. QBRs are also the primary venue for identifying
expansion or upsell opportunities, particularly when usage consistently exceeds the current plan's
limits or when a customer's health score has been consistently strong for two or more consecutive
quarters.
