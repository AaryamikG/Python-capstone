# Employee Handbook

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year during their first two years,
increasing to 20 days after two years and 25 days after five years. PTO accrues monthly and unused
days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days into the following year. Sick leave is tracked separately
and is not capped.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default; fully remote arrangements require
manager and HR approval. Employees are expected to be reachable during core hours (10am-3pm local
time) regardless of work location. Equipment stipends are available for employees setting up a home
office.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect, avoid conflicts of
interest, and report any violations to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against
anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself grounds for
disciplinary action.

## Performance Reviews and Employee Satisfaction

Formal performance reviews happen semi-annually, in June and December, combining self-assessment,
manager feedback, and peer feedback. Separately, an anonymous employee satisfaction survey is run every
quarter, covering topics like workload, management support, and career growth; department-level results
are shared with leadership and compared against industry benchmark data to identify areas needing
attention. Benefits, including health coverage and the 401(k) match, are reviewed annually and
communicated during open enrollment.
